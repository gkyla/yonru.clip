The brief

An intuitive platform that extracts high-virality hooks from long-form video. The visual identity had to feel elegant, soft, and technical using #ffd700 and #FFFFFF without cliché scissors or film brackets.

elegantsofttechnicaleffortless

Success: Distinct abstract mark, scales cleanly down to 16 px, zero cliché scissors/brackets, radiant in dark and light interfaces.
