Welcome to the initial release of **Yonru Clip**! Yonru is an AI-powered short video clipping and re-framing platform designed to automatically extract high-virality moments from long-form content and prepare them for modern short-form video formats.

### Features

- **AI Hook Discovery Engine**: Automatically scan YouTube videos to identify viral hooks with contextual AI Virality Rationale and scoring.
- **Smart Face Tracking & Auto-Reframe**: Intelligently crops 16:9 widescreen footage into 9:16 vertical formats, featuring single-speaker tracking and stacked multi-speaker reframe with facial coordinate clamping.
- **Multi-Track Studio Timeline**: Full-featured interactive timeline with dedicated tracks for video, audio, subtitles, and text overlays, backed by a decoupled viewport navigator.
- **Dynamic Subtitle Styling & Presets**: Built-in typography presets (Hormozi, Clean Vlog, and more) with real-time word-level animations, custom highlight colors, and pacing controls.
- **Word-Level Audio Censorship**: Seamlessly bleep or mute profanity and sensitive keywords with millisecond precision using the Acoustic Word Map without shifting video timing.
- **Platform Safe Zone Overlays**: Interactive preview overlays for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts to ensure captions and titles never get occluded by mobile UI elements.
- **Creator Cover Slide Studio**: Capture clean video frames, compose branded typography, and render custom title slides.
- **Local Whisper Transcription**: Hardware-guided transcription engine with GPU acceleration detection and memory safety guardrails.

### Improvements

- **Smooth Navigation Sidebar**: Collapsible 64px rail with quick access to cached videos, prompt templates, settings, and health diagnostics.
- **Global Command Palette**: Instant keyboard-driven navigation (`⌘K` / `Ctrl+K`) across videos, clips, and preferences.
- **Unified Launcher**: Self-healing runtime bootstrapper (`python run.py`) that checks environment requirements and launches services cleanly.

### Bug Fixes

- Fixed video playback desynchronization during backward timeline scrubbing.
- Corrected subtitle timestamp lead-time clamping to prevent boundary overflow.
- Resolved canvas letterbox clipping when switching between horizontal and vertical aspect ratios.
